NonDimensionalAnalysis — intent
================================

Goal
----
Determine how nonlinear the full mecanum plant is (LuGre–Adamov contact friction
included, not just the no-slip kinematic model) and, from that, decide for which
kinds of motion a linear controller suffices and for which motions a nonlinear /
robust controller is warranted.

Approach
--------
1. Non-dimensionalize every plant equation (body force/moment balances, wheel and
   roller torque balances, bristle rate equations, friction constitutive laws,
   contact-slip kinematics). Each equation's scaling factor is the physical
   quantity that equation balances (e.g. a characteristic force, torque, or rate).
2. From this, form dimensionless constants that each carry a clear physical
   meaning (e.g. spin-coupling strength, torque capacity vs. traction limit,
   bristle vs. body time scales, Stribeck speed vs. operating speed).
3. Vary these constants and measure the plant's nonlinearity across the
   resulting operating envelope.
4. Map the result onto classes of motion (speed, acceleration, spin, direction,
   friction level, ...) to identify:
     - regimes where the plant is close to linear -> linear control is adequate;
     - regimes where nonlinearity is significant -> nonlinear / robust control is
       warranted.

Source of the plant model: run_one.jl (dynamics_full_mf_asmc!, lugre_dyn_rates)
and trajectory_files_run_*/base.toml for parameter values.
